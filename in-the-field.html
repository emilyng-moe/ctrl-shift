💬 In the Field

Stories from MOE officers

Real people, real experiments, real results. No success theatre — just honest sharing.

HQ Officer
"The thing that surprised me most wasn't the output — it was how much mental energy I got back. AI handles the retrieval layer. I get to do the actual thinking."

Priya attended an M365 Copilot training session online — and came away with something she didn't expect: a shift in how she thinks about her own work. During the session, she used Copilot to summarise a 47-reply email thread live. What would have taken her 20 minutes of careful reading took seconds.

"I realised I'd been spending a lot of time just piecing together context," she says. "Finding the email, reading the chain, figuring out where things landed. Copilot just... does that. And then I can actually think about what to do next."

Her advice for officers who haven't tried it yet? "Don't wait for the right task. Just try it with something you're already working on."

👩‍💼
Priya Nair
Senior Executive · Curriculum Planning Division · MOE HQ

💬 In the Field

Stories from MOE officers

Real people, real experiments, real results. No success theatre — just honest sharing.

HQ Officer
"What stood out most to me was that Copilot delivers the greatest value when it is grounded in the right context ... As a result, I have been able to move beyond simple drafting tasks and embed AI into recurring work processes."

In University Policy Branch (UPB), under MOE's Higher Education Group, Ang Yong Qin's job involves piecing together historical context, synthesising information from multiple sources, and red-teaming policy options before recommendations reach management on how MOE works with the Autonomous Universities. What drew him to Copilot was its architecture — combining a Large Language Model with Retrieval-Augmented Generation to draw on organisational knowledge and web-based information, letting it synthesise, contextualise and reason across sources rather than just generate text from a blank prompt.

He got to put that thinking into practice when he attended the M365 Copilot workshops rolled out for HQ officers. The sessions reinforced how much of that value depends on context, so Yong Qin built reusable knowledge bases, skills and workflows that feed Copilot the right information every time — turning ad hoc prompts into something closer to a standing process, from summarising email chains and drafting correspondence to tracking outstanding actions across his portfolios.

The clearest example is his morning routine, where scheduled prompts pull together a consolidated view of his outstanding work straight from his inbox. "As Copilot understands the context behind each work item, it can consolidate the background, specific ask, actions taken, pending follow-ups, responsible stakeholders and upcoming deadlines into a single table," he says. "This gives me an immediate view of each matter's status and next steps, without manually reconstructing the full picture from multiple email threads."

His advice for colleagues just starting out? "Instead of trying to craft the perfect prompt upfront, work interactively with Copilot and guide it through the task step by step, refining the approach until it consistently produces what you need. Once the process works well, ask Copilot to turn those instructions into a reusable skill that you can apply again."

Ang Yong Qin
Senior Manager · Higher Education Group · UPB

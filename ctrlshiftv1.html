Use Case of the Month

Meeting Prep in Minutes with Copilot Notebook

Scattered email threads. Half-read documents. A meeting in 20 minutes. Sound familiar? Here's a 5-step workflow HQ officers are using to turn that chaos into a clean meeting brief — using Copilot Notebook at copilot.microsoft.com.

1

Open a new Notebook

Go to copilot.microsoft.com and start a new Notebook. This gives Copilot a persistent workspace — it remembers everything you paste in.

2

Paste in your raw materials

Drop in the relevant email threads, document excerpts, or meeting notes. Don't worry about formatting — just get the context in.

3

Ask for a structured brief

Prompt: "Summarise the key discussion points, decisions made, and open questions from the above. Format as a meeting brief."

4

Identify your role

Follow up with: "Based on the above, what should I be prepared to speak to or decide in this meeting?"

5

Walk in ready

You've got a brief, your talking points, and the key context — in under 10 minutes. The Notebook stays open so you can refer back during the meeting.

Try it before your next meeting

"Summarise the key discussion points, decisions made, and open questions from the above. Format as a meeting brief with a section on what I should be prepared to speak to."

Desk, Your New AI Assistant for Public Service Work
Tool of the Month · AIAssistant Desk

AIAssistant Desk has been quietly levelling up. If you haven't logged in recently, here's what's new — and why it's worth another look.

📧
Outlook integration
Read your emails, draft replies, summarise threads, and flag what needs action — without leaving Desk.
📅
Calendar access
Ask what's on your schedule, find a free slot, or prep for an upcoming meeting.
📄
File reading
Upload a PDF, Word doc, or spreadsheet and ask questions about it directly.
🌐
Web search
Get up-to-date information without switching tabs or leaving your workflow.
🧠
Memory across sessions
Desk remembers your preferences and context, so you don't have to repeat yourself every time.
📊
Data analysis & charts
Paste in data or upload a file and ask Desk to analyse it, spot trends, or generate a chart.

⚡ Quick Tip

Use Desk to prep before you write. Paste in your raw notes — bullet points, half-formed thoughts, email snippets — and ask: "What are the key points here?" Let it do the retrieval work so your brain can focus on the actual thinking.

🎯 Prompt of the Month

Pressure-Test My Position

Here is my current recommendation / draft position on [topic]: [paste draft]. Play devil's advocate — what are the strongest objections a sceptical stakeholder might raise? What have I not accounted for? What would make this argument more robust?

🎙 In the Field

Stories from MOE officers

Real people, real experiments, real results. No success theatre — just honest sharing.

HQ Officer
"The thing that surprised me most wasn't the output — it was how much mental energy I got back. AI handles the retrieval layer. I get to do the actual thinking."

Priya attended an M365 Copilot training session online — and came away with something she didn't expect: a shift in how she thinks about her own work. During the session, she used Copilot to summarise a 47-reply email thread live. What would have taken her 20 minutes of careful reading took seconds.

"I realised I'd been spending a lot of time just piecing together context," she says. "Finding the email, reading the chain, figuring out where things landed. Copilot just... does that. And then I can actually think about what to do next."

Her advice for officers who haven't tried it yet? "Don't wait for the right task. Just try it with something you're already working on."

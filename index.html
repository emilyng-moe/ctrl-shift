Welcome to ctrl+shift, a space for MOE officers to stay curious, stay connected, and stay ahead. No jargon. No overwhelm. Just the good stuff.

Get Started ↓

ctrl+shift is a shortcut, literally

On any keyboard, it's the combination behind switching modes, redoing what you just did, working a little differently. That's the idea here too: a small shift in how MOE officers work with AI, whatever your role or technical background.

We cut through the noise of a fast-moving field and surface what's clear, relevant, and actionable, so you can focus on what matters: doing your job better, with confidence.

Navigate AI with confidence

Understand which tools you can use, how to use them safely, and what the wider AI landscape looks like for public officers.

Stay connected to what matters

From MOE-specific updates to WOG announcements, ctrl+shift is your single source of curated AI news.

Grow at your own pace

Whether you're just AI-curious or already experimenting, every issue has something useful for you.

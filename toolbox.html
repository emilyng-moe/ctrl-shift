🎯 Prompt of the Month

Build a claims workflow with the Opus Assistant

When someone submits a claim through the form, the workflow should automatically do the following:

  1. Send the uploaded invoice to AiSay to read the document and extract important information, such as the invoice amount, date, vendor, and other relevant details.
  2. After AiSay has finished extracting the information, send the extracted details to an AI model to check the claim and prepare a simple summary.
  3. At the same time, while the invoice is being processed by AiSay, send an acknowledgement email to the applicant to let them know that their claim has been received.
  4. Also at the same time, send a Slack notification to the team to inform them that a new claim has been submitted.

The AiSay processing, acknowledgement email, and Slack notification should run in parallel so that the applicant and team do not have to wait for the invoice processing to finish. Once all these steps are complete, aggregate all the information and results together.

Finally, create a new case in OwnSelf Gather containing the claim details, information extracted from the invoice, AI validation and summary, and the relevant results from the workflow.

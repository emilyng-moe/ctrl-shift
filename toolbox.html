🎯 Prompt of the Month

Your Daily Inbox Triage Agent (Desk)

Every morning, check my Outlook inbox and identify emails that need a reply — prioritise anything unread, flagged, or from internal colleagues. For each email that warrants a response:

  1. Read the relevant context — search the specified SharePoint folder [insert folder name or URL] or the uploaded reference files I've provided, and use that content to ground your draft reply. Do not fabricate information that isn't in those sources.
  2. Draft a reply for each email. The draft should match my tone — direct and concise. Include a brief note at the top of each draft explaining which source(s) you drew from and why you chose that angle.
  3. Save every draft to my Outlook Drafts folder. Do not send anything. Present me with a summary of all drafts prepared, with a one-line description of each email and the approach taken.
  4. Wait for my explicit approval before any draft is sent. If I approve a specific draft, send it. If I ask for edits, revise and hold again before sending. Never send without a clear "send this" instruction from me.

If an email is ambiguous or the reference files don't contain enough information to draft a confident reply, flag it for my attention instead of guessing.
